## 🎯 THE 4 MAIN FEATURES

### 1. 🔍 SCANNER TAB

**What it does:** Finds tradeable stocks with liquid options

**How to use:**
1. Open Webull → Quotes → Gainers
2. See stocks up 10%+ (PTON, SAVA, AMD, etc.)
3. Go to Scanner tab in app
4. Type tickers: `PTON, SAVA, AMD`
5. Tap **"SCAN NOW"**
6. Wait 10-30 seconds
7. See results with ⭐ ratings

**What to look for:**
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5 stars) = EXCELLENT setup!
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4 stars) = Good setup
- ⭐⭐⭐ or less = Skip it

**For each result, you see:**
- Stock price and % gain
- Best option to buy (strike price, premium)
- Spread % (want <10%)
- Open Interest (want 100+)

**Actions:**
- Tap **"Details"** to see more info
- Tap **"Calculate"** to load into calculator

---

### 2. 💰 CALCULATOR TAB

**What it does:** Tells you HOW MANY contracts to buy

**How to use:**

**If you came from Scanner (recommended):**
- Stock info auto-filled! ✅
- Just tap **"CALCULATE POSITION"**
- See results instantly

**If entering manually:**
1. Enter ticker (PTON)
2. Enter stock price ($6.00)
3. Enter strike price ($5.00)
4. Enter option premium ($0.65)
5. Tap **"CALCULATE POSITION"**

**What you get:**
- **Recommended Contracts:** "Buy 4 contracts"
- **Total Cost:** "$260"
- **The Greeks:** Delta, Theta, etc.
- **Break-even:** "Stock must reach $6.15"
- **Profit Targets:**
  - If stock +10% → You make $200
  - If stock +20% → You make $600
  - If stock +30% → You make $1,200

**Look for:** ✅ "HITS 10% ACCOUNT GOAL!" marker

**Now you know:**
- HOW MANY: 4 contracts
- AT WHAT PRICE: $0.65
- WHAT STRIKE: $5.00
- MAX LOSS: $260

**Go trade it on Webull!**

---

### 3. 📒 JOURNAL TAB

**What it does:** Logs your trades

**How to use:**

**After your trade closes on Webull:**

1. Go to Journal tab
2. Tap **"➕ Add Trade"**
3. Fill out the form:

**Basic Info:**
- Ticker: PTON
- Strike: $5.00
- Contracts: 4
- Expiration: (pick date)

**Entry Details:**
- Entry Premium: $0.65
- Stock Price at Entry: $6.00
- Entry Time: 9:40 AM

**Exit Details:**
- Exit Premium: $1.45
- Stock Price at Exit: $6.35
- Exit Time: 9:55 AM
- Exit Reason: TARGET_HIT

**Strategy:**
- Pattern: Pullback
- MACD Positive: ✅
- Setup Quality: 5 stars
- Notes: (optional)

4. Tap **"💾 SAVE TRADE"**
5. See balloons! 🎉
6. Trade saved!

**View Your Trades:**
- Tap **"📊 View Trades"** tab
- See all your trades
- See win rate, total P&L
- Recent trades shown as cards

## 📅 DAILY WORKFLOW

### **Morning (7:00 AM - 9:30 AM)**

1. **Open Webull** → Find top gainers
2. **Open Trading App** → Scanner tab
3. **Enter tickers** from Webull
4. **Tap "Scan"** → wait for results
5. **Pick 5-star setup**
6. **Tap "Calculate"** → see position size
7. **Check news** (Webull News tab)
8. **Wait for market open** (9:30 AM)

### **Market Open (9:30 AM - 10:00 AM)**

9. **Watch 5-min chart** on Webull
10. **Wait for pullback pattern**:
    - Stock surges up
    - Pulls back (1-2 red candles)
    - Makes new high (green candle)
11. **Check MACD** is positive (green)
12. **Enter the trade** on Webull:
    - Buy 4 contracts (from calculator)
    - Use limit order
    - At premium shown in app

### **During Trade (5-30 minutes)**

13. **Watch for exit**:
    - Profit target hit ✅
    - MACD turns negative ❌
    - 30 minutes elapsed ⏰
    - Big red candle 📉
14. **Exit on first signal**
15. **Don't get greedy!**

### **After Trade (10:00 AM - 10:15 AM)**

16. **Open Trading App** → Journal tab
17. **Tap "Add Trade"**
18. **Fill out form** (takes 2 minutes)
19. **Tap "Save Trade"**
20. **DONE TRADING FOR THE DAY!** ✅

### **End of Week (Friday)**

21. **Open Dashboard** tab
22. **Check win rate** (target 75%)
23. **Check P/L ratio** (target 2:1)
24. **Read recommendations**
25. **Adjust strategy** for next week

## ❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

### "App is slow"
- Check WiFi connection
- Close other apps
- Refresh the page (swipe down)

### "Scanner shows no results"
- Tickers might not meet 5-pillar criteria
- Try different stocks from Webull gainers
- Make sure stocks are $2-$20 range, up 10%+

### "Calculator shows error"
- Make sure all fields filled out
- Stock price must be > 0
- Premium must be > 0

### "Can't save trade"
- Make sure all required fields filled
- Check internet connection
- Try again
